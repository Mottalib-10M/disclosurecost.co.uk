 1. Introduction
 This guidance helps you determine if the position you’re recruiting for is eligible for an Enhanced or Enhanced with Barred List DBS Check in the ‘other’ workforce.
 2. Roles eligible for Enhanced with both barred list checks
 Taxi drivers - When renewing or applying for a taxi or private hire vehicle licence, the licensing organisation can ask applicants to apply for an Enhanced DBS check with both Children’s and Adults’ Barred List checks. Applications by the licensing authority/organisation must be submitted in the Other workforce.  Taxi drivers are not in regulated activity with either children or adults when they submit this application for licensing purposes.
 Individuals applying through their local authority to be hosts for Ukrainian refugees that they are related to under the Homes for Ukraine scheme
 Individuals applying for or renewing a pedicab licence in London
 Registered healthcare professionals working for or on behalf of the Department for Work and Pensions to assess children or vulnerable adults
 3. Roles eligible for Enhanced only
 The below roles are eligible for an Enhanced DBS certificate, without access to a children’s or adult’s barred list check:
 Individuals applying for or renewing an operating licence to run premises where gambling can take place
 Individuals applying for or renewing a personal licence to perform the functions of a specified management office, or a specified operational function, in relation to facilities for gambling
 Individuals applying to work for the Gambling Commission
 Individuals applying for or renewing a licence to run the National Lottery or promote or sell tickets in relation to that lottery
 Individuals required to be registered by the Immigration Services Commissioner to provide immigration advice or services, not in connection with criminal proceedings
 Individuals applying for or renewing a licence to produce, supply, offer to supply or have in his possession any controlled drug
 Individuals applying to be a Maritime armed guard on UK ships where they can carry, acquire or transfer weapons and/or ammunition
 Individuals applying to be employed for the purpose of national security for the following:
 in service of the Crown;
 the United Kingdom Atomic Energy Authority
 the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA);
 the Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA)
 Individuals applying for judicial appointments, such as Judges, Magistrates, Coroners or Justices of the Peace
 Individuals applying to be employed by the Crown.
